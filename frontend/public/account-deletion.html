Delete your account & data

This page explains how to request deletion of your Sospana Sonke account and the personal information associated with it. It applies to the Sospana Sonke website and the Sospana Sonke Android app (package com.sospanasonke.app).

Option 1 — Delete it yourself (in the app)

  1. Open Sospana Sonke and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Data & Privacy.
  3. Choose Delete my account and confirm.

Your account and profile are removed immediately; backups are purged on our normal backup cycle (within 30 days).

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Email tshabalalalungani@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Sospana Sonke account". We verify it is you and complete the deletion within 7 working days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your candidate profile, uploaded CV(s) and AI-tailored CV versions
  • Cover letters, saved companies and application-tracking records
  • Personal contact details you provided

What we may keep, and for how long

We may retain a limited record where the law requires it (for example, billing/transaction records for tax and accounting) and anonymised, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you. Anything retained is kept only as long as legally required and is then deleted.

Sospana Sonke processes personal information in line with South Africa's POPIA. See our Privacy Policy. Questions: tshabalalalungani@gmail.com.
