How To Hustle across Gauteng

  • 🏃 Pick GUPTA, BIZA or LEBZA, then tap / Space to jump — your runner keeps running by themselves. Jump again in mid-air for a TRIPLE JUMP (3 pips under your feet)
  • 📍 You run through 34 areas of Gauteng — from the Vaal up through Johannesburg, Tshwane, Ekurhuleni and the West Rand — watch for the street signs and the “Now entering…” banner!
  • 🛞 Jump crates, barrels, rocks, spikes, pots and rolling tyres
  • 💰 Collect coins, gems & money bags. Grab ❤️ hearts for lives
  • 🔥 Chain pickups and near-misses for a COMBO — Sharp! Yebo! Lekker! (up to x5)
  • 🛹 Skateboard pick-up: ~8 seconds of fast, unstoppable rolling (and it turns up to rescue your runner on the last life)
  • 💥 Pulse blaster pick-up: toy blaster with limited ammo — it auto-fires at obstacles, or tap FIRE / press F
  • 🚕 Quantum Taxi: a few seconds of smash-everything speed
  • 🏪 SOSPANA SONKE shop appears now and then (Easy ~700 m, Normal ~850 m, Difficult ~1000 m) — run into the door or press ENTER for +2 LIVES
  • 🎚️ Three levels (pick on the start screen, ↑/↓ on a keyboard; each has its own best score):
    EASY — 5 lives, slow ramp, long grace time after a hit.
    NORMAL — 3 lives, faster, closer gaps, more obstacles.
    DIFFICULT — 2 lives, very fast, double obstacles, smaller rewards, rarer power-ups & shops — but your score counts x1.5.
  • 📴 Plays offline: open the game once, tap “Download for offline play”, and it works with no internet — add it to your Home Screen to install.
  • ❤️ GUPTA starts with 2–5 lives depending on the level — survive as far as you can!
