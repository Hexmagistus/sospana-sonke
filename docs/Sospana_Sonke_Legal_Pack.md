## 2. POPIA PRIVACY NOTICE

*(Draft — to be reviewed and finalised by a South African attorney. This notice is given to every candidate at or before the point their personal information is collected, per POPIA section 18.)*

**Effective date:** `[DATE]`
**Responsible party:** `[REGISTERED COMPANY NAME] ("Sospana Sonke", "we", "us")`, registration number `[CIPC REG NO]`, of `[REGISTERED ADDRESS]`.
**Information Officer:** `[NAME]`, `[EMAIL]`, `[PHONE]` (registered with the Information Regulator of South Africa).

### 2.1 Who we are and why this notice exists
Sospana Sonke operates an online platform that helps job seekers discover vacancies advertised on employers' official careers pages, assess their fit, generate tailored CVs and cover letters, and prepare or submit job applications. To do this we must process your personal information. This notice explains what we collect, why, how we protect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (**POPIA**).

### 2.2 The personal information we process
We process the information you give us and information generated as you use the platform, including: your **identity and contact details** (name, surname, email address, mobile number); **profile information** (city, country, work authorisation, current and desired occupations, industries, salary expectations, location and remote preferences, willingness to relocate); **education, certifications, and work history**; **skills, languages, driver's licence, professional memberships, and links** (portfolio, LinkedIn, GitHub); the **CV documents you upload** and the **tailored CVs and cover letters** we generate for you; your **application records and their status**; your **account settings and autonomy preferences**; **subscription and payment status** (we do **not** store your full card details — those are handled by our payment provider); and **technical/usage data** (log-in times, device and usage logs) used for security and to operate the service.

Some of this may be **special personal information** or otherwise sensitive (for example, information that could reveal health, or your South African ID number if you choose to provide it). We only process such information where POPIA permits it and where it is necessary for a job application you have asked us to help with, and we apply extra safeguards.

### 2.3 Why we process it (purpose)
We process your information to: create and manage your account; build and maintain your candidate profile; match your profile against vacancies; generate tailored CVs, cover letters, and reports; prepare and — **only with your authorisation** — submit job applications; keep you informed (notifications); manage your R100/month subscription; provide support; keep the platform secure; and comply with the law. **We do not use your information for any purpose incompatible with these, and we do not sell your personal information.**

### 2.4 Lawful basis
We rely on: your **consent** (given at sign-up and, separately, for automated/assisted applications); the **performance of our contract** with you (to deliver the service you subscribe to); our **legitimate interests** (platform security, fraud prevention, service improvement) balanced against your rights; and **compliance with legal obligations**.

### 2.5 Who we share it with (operators and third parties)
We share information only as needed to run the service, with vendors acting as our **operators** under written contracts that require them to protect your information and process it only on our instructions. These include our **cloud hosting** provider, **AI** provider(s) (used to interpret vacancies and generate documents), **email** provider, and **payment** provider. When you authorise an application, the tailored CV, cover letter, and the application answers you approve are submitted **to the relevant employer or its recruitment system** — that is the whole point of the service, and it happens only with your authorisation. We may also disclose information if required by law.

### 2.6 Cross-border transfers
Some operators may process information **outside South Africa**. Where this happens, we ensure a lawful basis under POPIA section 72 — typically your consent and/or a contract that requires the recipient to uphold protections comparable to POPIA. Our current processing locations are described at `[LINK / APPENDIX]`. **⚖️ ATTORNEY: confirm each vendor's processing location and the transfer mechanism relied on.**

### 2.7 How long we keep it (retention)
We keep your information only as long as necessary (see the retention schedule in §8). In summary: while your account is active and for a defined period afterwards for legal, audit, and dispute purposes, after which it is deleted or anonymised. **We do not keep your uploaded documents indefinitely.**

### 2.8 How we protect it
We apply encryption in transit and at rest, strong password hashing, access controls on a least-privilege basis, malware scanning of uploads, audit logging, and regular backups. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable, appropriate, and up-to-date measures as POPIA requires.

### 2.9 Your rights
Under POPIA you may: **access** the personal information we hold about you; **correct or update** it; **object** to processing or **withdraw consent** (which may mean we can no longer provide the service); request **deletion** of your information; and request a **copy/export** of your data. You may exercise these rights via `[EMAIL]` or your account's *Data & Privacy* settings. You also have the right to **complain to the Information Regulator**.

**Information Regulator (South Africa)** — website: https://inforegulator.org.za · complaints email: `POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za` · general: `enquiries@inforegulator.org.za`. **⚖️ ATTORNEY: verify current Regulator contact details at time of publication.**

### 2.10 Automated decision-making
Our platform uses AI to *suggest* which vacancies fit you and to *draft* documents, but **a human — you — remains the decision-maker**. Nothing is submitted to an employer without your authorisation. You have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that has legal or similarly significant effects; our human-in-the-loop design is intended to honour this.

### 2.11 Changes
We may update this notice; we will post the new version and, for material changes, notify you.

## 4. CANDIDATE AUTHORISATION FOR AUTOMATED & ASSISTED APPLICATIONS

*(Draft — this is a **separate, explicit** authorisation, presented on its own and agreed to independently of general Terms, because it authorises us to act on the candidate's behalf. It should be re-confirmed when the candidate enables automatic mode.)*

**This authorisation is given by:** `[Candidate name, captured at agreement]` ("you") **to** Sospana Sonke.

**1. What you are authorising.** You authorise Sospana Sonke to prepare job applications on your behalf using the information in your profile and the CVs and cover letters generated for you, and — **depending on the application mode you choose** — to assist you in submitting them or to submit them for you, to the employers and recruitment systems associated with the vacancies you are matched to.

**2. The three modes, and your control.** You choose one of:
   - **Assisted** — we prepare and pre-fill as much as possible; **you complete and submit** every application yourself.
   - **Approval-required** — we prepare a complete application and **submit it only after you review and approve that specific application**.
   - **Automatic** — for vacancies that meet the criteria you set (minimum match score, caps, included/excluded employers and roles), and **only where the employer's system permits automated submission and no human step (such as CAPTCHA, multi-factor authentication, or login) is required**, we may submit on your behalf without asking each time.

   You may change your mode, pause, or withdraw this authorisation at any time in your settings. Withdrawal takes effect going forward and does not reverse applications already submitted.

**3. Truthfulness — our binding commitment.** We will **never fabricate, exaggerate, or misrepresent** any information about you — including qualifications, employment history, job titles, certifications, professional registrations, skills, references, salary, achievements, work authorisation, experience, or academic results. Where an application asks a factual question we cannot answer from your confirmed profile, we will **mark it as requiring your input** rather than guess. **You confirm that the information in your profile is true and complete**, and you remain responsible for its accuracy.

**4. What we will not do.** We will not bypass CAPTCHA, multi-factor authentication, log-in walls, or any anti-automation or security control on an employer's system; we will not access anything requiring credentials you have not authorised us to use; and we will not submit applications you have not authorised. Where an employer's system prevents automated submission, we will fall back to assisted mode and tell you exactly what to do.

**5. Limits of what we can promise.** Authorising applications does **not** guarantee interviews, responses, or employment. Employers make all hiring decisions. Some employer systems may not support automated or assisted submission at all.

**6. Records.** We keep a record of each application prepared or submitted on your behalf, the information supplied, and the date and time, available to you in your dashboard and reports.

## 5. AI USAGE DISCLOSURE

*(Draft — shown at sign-up and linked in-product wherever AI output appears.)*

Sospana Sonke uses artificial intelligence to make your job search faster and better targeted. Here is what that means and what it does not.

**What the AI does:** it reads vacancy advertisements and organises them into structured information; it compares your profile to each vacancy and produces a match score with a plain-language explanation of why you do or do not appear to qualify; it drafts CVs tailored to specific vacancies and cover letters; and it helps interpret application questions and prepare reports.

**What the AI does not do:** it does not make final decisions for you, it does not submit anything without your authorisation, it does not decide your billing or account status, and — critically — **it does not invent facts about you.** Tailored documents are built only from information you have confirmed in your profile.

**Its limits:** AI matching is **advisory and can be wrong**. A high score is not a promise of an interview or a job, and a low score does not mean you should not apply. You should review everything the AI produces before it is used. Employers, not Sospana Sonke, make all hiring decisions.

**Explainability:** for important AI outputs we keep a record of the decision, the reason, a confidence level, the time, and the model and prompt version used, and we show you a concise explanation. You can ask us about any AI-assisted decision that affects you.

**Human control:** you can review, edit, approve, or reject any AI output, and you can reduce the platform's autonomy at any time.

## 7. Operator (data-processing) clause checklist for vendors

Every vendor that processes candidate personal information on your behalf (cloud host, AI provider, email provider, payment provider, error-tracking, backups) must be bound by a written **Operator Agreement** (POPIA sections 20–21) that, at minimum: (a) limits the operator to processing **only on your documented instructions**; (b) requires **appropriate, up-to-date security safeguards** and notification to you of any compromise **without undue delay**; (c) prohibits **further processing** for the operator's own purposes; (d) addresses **cross-border processing** and the lawful basis for it; (e) requires **assistance** with data-subject requests and with your POPIA obligations; (f) covers **sub-operators** (the vendor's own subcontractors) and requires equivalent terms; (g) requires **return or deletion** of personal information at the end of the relationship; and (h) permits **audit/verification** of compliance. Many vendors publish a standard Data Processing Addendum (DPA) — collect and file each one, and have your attorney confirm it meets POPIA. **⚖️ ATTORNEY: review each vendor DPA against POPIA sections 20–21.**
